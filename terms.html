What LotHQ does

LotHQ is software that helps properties manage parking: permits tied to license plates, unit spots, guest passes, Pay to Park, and plate checks by enforcement. LotHQ does not own or operate the parking lots, set their rules or prices, or tow or ticket vehicles.

Parking rules and enforcement

  • Each property sets its own parking rules, prices and enforcement, shown in its Parking Terms. When you add a vehicle, give a guest pass or pay to park, you agree to that property's Parking Terms.
  • Decisions to tow, boot, ticket or warn are made by the property and its enforcement company under their rules and the law, not by LotHQ.
  • Questions about a tow, ticket, refund or your parking should go to the property manager or the enforcement company named on the property's signs.

Your responsibilities

  • Enter accurate information, especially your license plate. Permits and paid time are tied to the plate you enter; a wrong plate may not be recognized.
  • Keep your vehicles and passes up to date, for example when you change cars or move out.
  • Keep your password private, and tell us or your manager if you think someone else is using your account.
  • Only add vehicles and guests you're allowed to, and use guest passes for real guests.

Payments

  • Payments are processed by Stripe and are subject to Stripe's terms. Paid parking is paid to the property (or the business it chooses) unless the property tells you otherwise.
  • Prices, billing schedules and refund decisions are set by the property. Ask the property manager for refunds.
  • Paid time starts when your payment goes through. Recurring spot payments renew until cancelled through the property.
