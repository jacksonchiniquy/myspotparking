Property Manager
Complete control over every property

Issue permits, manage residents, build lot maps, track violations, and monitor revenue — from one dashboard.

  • Visual drag-and-drop lot map editor
  • Resident management with stall assignment
  • Permit issuance and revocation
  • Violation logging and reporting
  • Multi-property support
Manager Portal
Resident

  • Digital permit accessible anytime
  • Register and manage multiple vehicles
  • Guest pass requests
  • Account and subscription self-management

Features

The tools that actually
matter for parking.

No feature bloat. No hardware required. Just the software that makes your operation run.

Visual Lot Map Editor

Drag-and-drop canvas with snap-to-grid, zoom levels, satellite photo backgrounds, and multi-stall selection.

AI Layout Generation

Upload a satellite photo or describe your lot — AI generates a starting layout in seconds for you to edit.

Digital Permit Issuance

Issue permits instantly to residents by email or internal roster. No stickers, no printing, no mailing.

Resident Management

Full resident roster with unit numbers, stall assignments, vehicle info, permit history, and active/inactive status.

Violation Logging

Enforcement logs violations by plate with reason, timestamp, and property. Searchable and exportable by managers.

Dashboard & Reporting

Occupancy rates, active permits, monthly revenue, and violation trends — all at a glance per property.

Stripe Payments

Monthly, quarterly, and annual permit subscriptions. Automatic renewals and revenue tracking built in.

Multi-Property Support

Manage unlimited properties under one account. Each property has its own permits, map, residents, and reports.

Works on Any Device

Every portal is mobile-ready. Residents on their phones. Enforcement in the field. Managers at their desk.
