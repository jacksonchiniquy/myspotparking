How you park

Four ways to run parking.
Use one or mix them.

Every property is different. Pick the setup that matches how your lot works today. You don't need to change your operation to fit the software.

Permits by plate

Each resident or tenant gets a digital permit tied to their license plate. Enforcement checks the plate and sees the permit.

Best for open lots and general resident parking

Assigned spaces

Draw your lot on the map and assign stalls to people. See at a glance what's taken, open, reserved, EV or accessible.

Best for reserved, covered and numbered stalls

Unit-based

Parking belongs to the unit. Residents sign in with their unit and swap the car on each spot themselves.

Best for apartments, condos and HOAs

Pay to Park

Visitors scan a sign and pay by the hour from their phone. No app and no account. Enforcement sees the paid time instantly.

Best for visitor, overflow and commercial lots
Mix and match: one property can use unit-based parking for residents and Pay to Park for visitors, while another uses plate permits. Each property is set up on its own.
Pay to Park

Turn open stalls into revenue.

Post a sign with a QR code. Drivers pay for the time they need from their phone, and you get paid through Stripe.

  1. Scan the signThe QR code opens your lot's payment page. Nothing to download.
  2. Pick a time and payThe driver enters their plate, chooses a rate you set, and pays by card.
  3. Get a receiptAn emailed receipt shows the plate, start time and when parking ends.
  4. Enforcement sees it right awayA plate lookup shows the paid session and when it expires.

Fair enforcement

No tows over a typo.

People mix up characters like 5 and S, O and 0, or 1 and I when they enter a plate. When a plate is close to a valid permit, we flag it for the officer instead of calling it a violation.

  • The officer sees both plates side by side
  • A person decides, never the system
  • Your manager is told to correct the plate
  • Fewer disputes and wrongful tows

Questions

Common questions

Do residents or drivers need to download an app?

No. Everything works in a phone's web browser. Residents sign in from a link, and Pay to Park drivers just scan the sign.

Do we still need parking stickers or hang tags?

No. Permits are tied to the license plate, so enforcement checks the plate itself.

What happens if someone typed their plate wrong?

If a plate is close to a valid permit, the officer sees a possible match and decides. Nobody gets a violation from a typo alone, and the manager is asked to fix the plate.

Can residents update their own vehicles?

Yes. Residents can add vehicles and swap the car on their spot. You can lock any spot so only the manager can change it.

How do residents pay for parking?

Paid spots are billed monthly, quarterly or yearly by card through Stripe. Free spots need no payment. Residents choose which paid spots they want when they sign up.

Can we use our own enforcement company?

Yes. We set up your enforcement company and assign them to your properties. They only see the properties you've assigned.

How long does setup take?

Our team handles setup with you. We load your properties, units and residents so you're ready from day one.
