Our commitment

LotHQ wants every resident, tenant, guest, driver, manager and officer to be able to use our website and portals, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we do

  • Pages work on phones, tablets and computers, and can be zoomed without losing content.
  • Text and buttons are designed for readable color contrast.
  • Forms have labels, and most features can be used with a keyboard.
  • Training videos include captions.
  • Resident, tenant, guest and driver pages are available in 8 languages from the language menu.

Known limits

Some parts of LotHQ may not fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA yet, such as the lot map drawing tool and pages provided by others, like Stripe's payment pages. We review LotHQ regularly and fix problems as we find them.

Report a problem or get help

If something on LotHQ is hard to use, or you need information in another format, email support@lothq.com. Tell us the page and what happened, and we'll help you park or get what you need while we work on a fix. We try to respond within 2 business days.
