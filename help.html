How can we help?

Answers for residents, visitors, property managers and enforcement.

Residents

For residents with their own LotHQ login (email and password). If your parking is set up by unit with a unit password, see Unit tenants.

Getting started

Do I need to download an app?

No. LotHQ works in your phone’s web browser. Sign in at lothq.com/login with your email and password.

I didn’t get my welcome email.

Check your spam or junk folder for an email from LotHQ. Still nothing? Ask your property manager to confirm the email address they have for you.

I forgot my password.

Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in page. A reset link is emailed to you.

Vehicles and permits

Do I need a sticker or hang tag?

No. Your permit is tied to your license plate. The enforcement company checks the plate itself.

How do I add or change a vehicle?

Sign in, open My Vehicles, tap + Add Vehicle and add the plate, make, model and color. Double-check the plate; it’s what enforcement looks up.

I got a new car. What do I do?

Add the new car and remove the old one before you park. The change is live for enforcement right away.

Why do I have to check a box agreeing to Parking Terms?

Your property’s parking rules apply to every car you add. The checkbox records that you saw them.

Billing

How do I pay for a paid spot?

Paid spots are billed by card through Stripe, monthly, quarterly or yearly, as your property sets it up. Free spots need no payment.

How do I update my card or see receipts?

Open Subscription, then Manage Billing. Stripe’s secure billing page opens, where you can change your card and see past payments.

My payment failed. Will I lose my spot?

You’ll get an email to update your card. If billing stays unpaid, the permit can be suspended. Update your card in Manage Billing as soon as you can.

How do I cancel a paid spot?

Contact your property manager. Cancelling a paid spot means that car is no longer covered.

Guests and towing

How do my guests park?

If your property offers guest passes, they’re sent from your unit’s portal (see Unit tenants). Your property sets how long a pass lasts and how many you get. Not sure? Ask your property manager.

My car was towed or ticketed. Who do I contact?

Contact your property manager first. LotHQ keeps a record of plate checks and actions, so they can see what happened.

Who can see my information?

Your property’s managers and, for plate checks only, the enforcement company assigned to your property. Card details stay with Stripe; LotHQ never stores them.

Unit tenants

For tenants whose parking is set up by unit. One login per unit, shared by everyone in it.

Signing up and signing in

How do I sign up?

Your property manager emails an invite for your unit. Open it, follow the sign-up link, choose any paid spots you want and finish. A welcome email follows with your unit password.

My invite expired or I can’t find it.

Ask your property manager to send it again. They can resend it anytime before you sign up.

How do I sign in?

Go to lothq.com/login, find your property, then enter your unit number and the unit password.

I forgot the unit password.

Use “Forgot password” on the unit sign-in screen. A reset is sent to the email on file for your unit.

Spots and vehicles

How do I put a car in a spot?

Open your unit, choose the spot and add the car’s plate, make, model and color. You’ll check a box agreeing to the Parking Terms.

I got a new car or a roommate’s car changed.

Swap the car on that spot. The old plate stops being valid and the new one starts right away.

I can’t change a spot.

Your manager may have locked it. Ask them to make the change.

How do I add an extra paid spot?

Use the add-a-spot option in your unit portal, or the payment link your manager emails you. It’s billed monthly by card.

How do I update my card or cancel a paid spot?

Open Account, then Manage Billing, to update your card. To cancel a paid spot, contact your property manager.

Guest passes

How do I give a guest a pass?

If your property offers guest passes, open Guest Passes in your unit portal, choose the pass type and send it. Your guest gets a code to activate with their plate.

How long does a pass last, and how many do I get?

Your property sets both. Your portal shows how many you have left.

Does a pass end on its own?

Yes. It ends at the time shown. Guests who get their pass by email are reminded about an hour before it ends.

Moving out

What happens when we move out?

Your manager closes out the unit. Card billing for the unit stops, your cars are removed from the permit list and the next tenant gets a new invite.

Pay to Park

For visitors paying to park at a LotHQ sign. No account or app needed.

Paying

How do I pay?

Scan the QR code on the sign, or go to lothq.com/park and type the lot code from the sign. Choose how long you’re staying, enter your plate and pay by card.

Do I need to download an app or make an account?

No. It all works in your phone’s web browser.

What’s the stall number for?

Some lots ask for the stall number printed on the sign. If the QR code is on your stall’s sticker, it’s filled in for you.

Why do I check a box before paying?

It confirms you agree to the property’s Parking Terms, the rules for parking there.

After you pay

Do I get a receipt?

Yes. Enter your email when you pay and a receipt is sent. The confirmation screen also shows your plate and when your time ends.

When does my time start?

When your payment goes through, not when you open the page.

Do I need to put anything on my dashboard?

No. Your parking is tied to your plate, and enforcement checks the plate.

Can I add more time?

Start a new payment for the extra time before your current time ends.

Problems

I typed my plate wrong.

Contact the property manager right away with the correct plate and your receipt. If a plate is close to a paid one, the officer sees a possible match before taking action.

My payment went through twice, or I want a refund.

Contact the property manager with your emailed receipt. They handle refunds for their lot.

The page says payments are paused.

The property is finishing its payment setup. Park somewhere else or contact the property manager.

My car was towed or ticketed.

Contact the property manager or the towing company named on the sign, and have your receipt ready.

Managers

For property managers and their staff.

Getting set up

How do I get a manager account?

Request access at lothq.com. LotHQ approves it and emails you. Setup is free for standard properties.

What do I need to send LotHQ?

A unit list in our spreadsheet template, usually from a rent roll export in Yardi, Entrata or similar. We need unit numbers, spot counts and tenant names and emails, never financial data.

Does LotHQ connect to Yardi or Entrata?

Not yet. Connections are on our roadmap; tell us which software you use.

Can I manage several properties?

Yes. All your properties are in one login, each with its own settings.

Units and tenants

How do tenants get access?

You send an invite from the Units tab. Each unit gets one invite; you can resend it anytime before they sign up.

Can I stop tenants from changing a car?

Yes. Lock the spot so only you can change it.

What happens when a tenant moves out?

Use Move Out on the unit. Card billing stops, their cars come off the permit list, their history is saved and a fresh invite is ready for the next tenant.

Some spots are included in rent. Can I mark them paid?

Yes, if LotHQ has turned on “paid outside the app” for your property. Ask us.

Payments and payouts

Where does parking money go?

To whoever you choose for each property: the HOA, the management company or the owner. Set it up in the property’s Payouts tab through Stripe’s secure page. LotHQ never sees bank details.

When do deposits arrive?

On Stripe’s normal schedule, usually 2 business days after a payment.

Why are payments paused at a property?

Payout setup was started but isn’t finished. Open Payouts and click Continue setup with Stripe.

How does LotHQ charge us?

As written in your agreement: a subscription, a fee per payment, or both. Plans are month-to-month; cancel with 30 days’ notice.

Who handles refunds?

You do, from your Stripe Dashboard. Payments are made in your payout account.

Rules and enforcement

Can we keep our towing company?

Yes. LotHQ sets them up and assigns them to your properties. They only see the properties you assign.

What if a plate is typed wrong?

The officer sees a possible match and decides. You’re asked to fix the plate.

Where are the parking rules?

In the Parking Terms everyone agrees to when they add a plate. LotHQ fills in your owner, management company and property rules.

Can I see who changed what?

Yes. Every change is logged with who made it and when.

Enforcement

For towing and enforcement officers.

Getting in

How do I get an account?

Your company is set up by LotHQ and assigned to the properties you patrol. You’ll get a sign-in email.

Which properties can I check?

Only the ones assigned to your company. Ask the property manager or LotHQ if one is missing.

Do I need an app?

No. Sign in at lothq.com on your phone’s web browser: Business Login, then Enforcement.

Checking plates

How do I check a car?

Pick the property, type the plate and check. LotHQ looks at resident permits, unit spots, guest passes and Pay to Park at once.

What do the results mean?

Green (valid permit, guest pass or paid session): the car is allowed. Amber (possible match): a plate is close to a valid one, likely a typo; compare the car before acting. Red (no valid permit): nothing covers this plate; act per the property’s rules.

Does the car need a sticker or hang tag?

No. Permits are tied to the plate.

Taking action

How do I log a tow, boot or ticket?

After a no-permit result, choose the action, add a note and a photo if you can, and save. Only the actions the property allows show up.

Who sees what I log?

The property’s managers see every action, with time, plate and your name.

A resident says they paid or just changed cars.

Check the plate again; changes are live right away. If it’s still not covered, follow the property’s rules and note what they told you.
