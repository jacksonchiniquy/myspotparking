Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

The short version: LotHQ collects what's needed to run parking at your property: your name and contact details, your vehicles and plates, permits, guest passes and payments. Your property and its assigned enforcement company can see it for their parking program. Card numbers stay with Stripe. We don't sell your information, track your location, or use it for ads.

Who we are

LotHQ provides online parking permit, guest pass, Pay to Park and parking enforcement tools for HOAs, property managers and property owners ("properties"). In this policy, "LotHQ," "we" and "us" mean LotHQ LLC, a Utah limited liability company. Questions: support@lothq.com.

This policy covers lothq.com and the LotHQ portals and pages for managers, residents, unit tenants, guests, Pay to Park drivers and enforcement officers. LotHQ is offered in the United States, and your information is stored in the United States.

Who controls your information

We provide LotHQ to properties. When you park at a LotHQ property, the property (or the company that manages it) decides which information it collects about you and how it uses it for its parking program. We handle that information on the property's behalf. If you have a question about your parking, a permit, a tow or a refund, your property manager is usually the right first contact.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, company, and password (stored by our login provider in protected form, never visible to us). If you choose "Continue with Google," Google shares your name and email address with us.
  • Parking details: property, unit or apartment number, parking spot, license plate, vehicle make, model and color, permit dates, guest pass details, and your agreement to the property's Parking Terms (with the plate, the version of the terms and the date and time).
  • Pay to Park details: license plate, stall number when asked, parking time, any promo code, and the email you give for a receipt.
  • Enforcement records: plate checks made by officers at your property, and any tow, boot or ticket they log, with the time and the officer's name.
  • Payment details: handled by Stripe. We receive limited information such as the amount, date, status and the last four digits of a card. We never receive or store full card numbers or bank account numbers.
  • Property setup: unit lists that properties send or import, and lot images managers upload to draw a lot map.
  • Messages: what you send us, such as support emails or the contact form.
  • Usage and device information: sign-ins, changes made in the portals (our activity log records who changed what and when), and basic technical data such as IP address and browser type, which our hosting provider may also log.

We don't collect your location from your phone (GPS), and we don't buy information about you from data brokers.

How we use it

  • To run the service: issue and check permits, guest passes and Pay to Park sessions, and show properties and enforcement who is allowed to park.
  • To send service emails: invites, sign-in and password links, receipts, guest pass notices, renewal reminders and account notices.
  • To process payments for the property through Stripe.
  • To keep LotHQ secure, prevent misuse and fraud, fix problems, and keep records of changes.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We don't sell your personal information, we don't share it for advertising, and we don't use it to show you ads.

Who can see it

  • Your property's managers and staff: the information for their property.
  • Enforcement companies assigned to your property: they can check whether a plate is allowed to park and log actions. They see what they need for that, not your full account.
  • Service providers that run LotHQ for us: Supabase (database, logins and file storage), Netlify (website hosting), Amazon Web Services (email delivery), Stripe (payments), Google (only if you choose "Continue with Google") and Anthropic (an AI service that helps managers draw a lot map from a lot image they upload; it doesn't receive resident information). They may use information only to provide their services to us.
  • Law enforcement and legal requests: only when required by a subpoena, court order, warrant or other legal process, or when we believe in good faith it's needed to protect someone's safety in an emergency.
  • A buyer or successor: if LotHQ is part of a merger, sale or reorganization, information may transfer, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

Cookies and browser storage

LotHQ uses your browser's storage only to keep you signed in and to remember a few choices, such as your language, the property you last signed in to, and the property an officer is patrolling. We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics. Stripe may use its own cookies on its secure payment pages to prevent fraud. You can clear your browser's storage at any time, but you'll need to sign in again.

How long we keep it

  • While a property uses LotHQ: we keep its information as long as it's needed to run its parking program.
  • Move-outs: when a tenant moves out, the property closes out the unit. The unit's history (past tenants, vehicles, permits and guest passes) is kept so the property has a record.
  • Activity log, payment and enforcement records: kept long-term, because properties, enforcement companies and LotHQ may need them for disputes, accounting or the law.
  • When a property stops using LotHQ: we delete its data within 90 days after its service ends, except information we must keep for legal, tax, accounting or dispute reasons. Stripe keeps its own payment records under its own policies.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules that limit each person to the properties and records they're allowed to see, protected password storage, private storage for files, and a log of changes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it to yourself. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll notify you and the property as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can view and update most of your information in your portal. Wherever you live in the United States, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we have about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that's wrong;
  • delete your information.

To make a request, email support@lothq.com or contact your property manager. We'll confirm your identity (usually by email) before acting, and we'll respond within 45 days, or tell you if we need more time. You can have someone else make a request for you if you give them written permission. If we handle your information for a property, we may send your request to the property or work with it to respond. Some information may need to be kept, for example records of payments or enforcement actions, while the property needs them or the law requires it. If we turn down a request, you can ask us to reconsider by replying to our answer.

We don't sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of there. We won't treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

We only send service emails needed for your account or parking. If you reply to one of our emails, your reply goes to our support inbox.

Children

LotHQ is meant for adults. Accounts are for people 18 and older. Drivers who are 16 or 17 may use Pay to Park and guest passes with a parent or guardian's permission. LotHQ is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as LotHQ changes. We'll change the date at the top, and for significant changes we'll give notice in the portals or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: support@lothq.com.
