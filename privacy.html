Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

The short version: LotHQ collects what's needed to run parking at your property: your name and contact details, your vehicles and plates, permits, guest passes and payments. Your property and its assigned enforcement company can see it for their parking program. Card numbers stay with Stripe. We don't sell your information or use it for ads.

Who we are

LotHQ provides online parking permit, guest pass, Pay to Park and parking enforcement tools for HOAs, property managers and property owners ("properties"). In this policy, "LotHQ," "we" and "us" mean LotHQ, based in Utah, USA. Questions: support@myspotparking.com.

This policy covers lothq.com and the LotHQ portals and pages for managers, residents, unit tenants, guests, Pay to Park drivers and enforcement officers.

Who controls your information

We provide LotHQ to properties. When you park at a LotHQ property, the property (or the company that manages it) decides which information it collects about you and how it uses it for its parking program. We handle that information on the property's behalf. If you have a question about your parking, a permit, a tow or a refund, your property manager is usually the right first contact.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, company, and password (stored by our login provider in protected form, never visible to us).
  • Parking details: property, unit or apartment number, parking spot, license plate, vehicle make, model and color, permit dates, guest pass details, and your agreement to the property's Parking Terms (with the date and time).
  • Pay to Park details: license plate, stall number when asked, parking time, and the email you give for a receipt.
  • Enforcement records: plate checks made by officers at your property, and any tow, boot, ticket or warning they log, including notes and photos they add.
  • Payment details: handled by Stripe. We receive limited information such as the amount, date, status and the last four digits of a card. We never receive or store full card numbers.
  • Messages: what you send us, such as support emails or the contact form.
  • Usage and device information: sign-ins, changes made in the portals (our activity log records who changed what and when), and basic technical data such as browser type, which our hosting provider may also log.

How we use it

  • To run the service: issue and check permits, guest passes and Pay to Park sessions, and show properties and enforcement who is allowed to park.
  • To send service emails: invites, sign-in and password links, receipts, guest pass notices and account notices.
  • To process payments for the property through Stripe.
  • To keep LotHQ secure, prevent misuse, fix problems, and keep records of changes.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Your property's managers and staff: the information for their property.
  • Enforcement companies assigned to your property: they can check whether a plate is allowed to park and log actions. They see what they need for that, not your full account.
  • Service providers that run LotHQ for us: Supabase (database and logins), Netlify (website hosting), Amazon Web Services (email delivery) and Stripe (payments). They may use the information only to provide their services.
  • Others when required: if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or rights, or as part of a merger or sale of our business (in which case this policy would continue to apply).

How long we keep it

We keep information while the property uses LotHQ and as long as needed for the purposes above. When a tenant moves out, the property can close out the unit; history may be kept so the property has a record of past permits and enforcement. When a property stops using LotHQ, we delete or return its data within a reasonable time unless we need to keep some of it for legal, tax or dispute reasons.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules that limit each person to the properties and records they're allowed to see, protected password storage, and a log of changes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it to yourself.

Your choices

You can view and update most of your information in your portal. To ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion, contact your property manager or email support@myspotparking.com. Some information may need to be kept, for example records of payments or enforcement actions, while the property needs them or the law requires it.

We only send service emails needed for your account or parking. If you reply to one of our emails, your reply goes to our support inbox.

Children

LotHQ is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as LotHQ changes. We'll change the date at the top, and for significant changes we'll give notice in the portals or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: support@myspotparking.com.
